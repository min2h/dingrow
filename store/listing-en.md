## Full description

Dingrow is a small pet that lives on your home screen. It reminds you when a task is due, and it grows when you finish one.

Feed it, watch fullness and happiness, and send it on a walk once it is a child. Sell the gifts it brings back, then spend the coins on clothes and furniture. The friend dex has many species you can bring home.

What you can do

- Add, complete, snooze for 10 minutes, or delete a task. Repeat once, daily, or weekly
- Grow from an egg to a baby, a child, and an adult
- A small widget (today's mood, the next task, complete) and a large widget (feed, gifts, shop, room)
- Sleeps at night, and the live wallpaper shows the same room
- Saved on the device only, with no account

Dingrow has no ads, and the pet does not leave when it is hungry.

On a Korean device the name under the icon is 딩그로. Everywhere else it is Dingrow.
